## Regulatory Landscape

### HIPAA Requirements for AI (2025)

**Key Compliance Mandates:**

1. **Minimum Necessary Standard**
   - AI must access only PHI strictly necessary
   - Challenge: AI models want comprehensive datasets
   - **Nillion Advantage:** TEE enables full data access without exposure

2. **Business Associate Agreements (BAAs)**
   - All AI vendors processing PHI need robust BAAs
   - Must outline permissible data use and safeguards
   - **Nillion Advantage:** TEE provides technical safeguard (not just contractual)

3. **De-identification Requirements**
   - Must meet Safe Harbor or Expert Determination standards
   - Risk of re-identification when datasets combined
   - **Nillion Advantage:** Can work with identified data securely (TEE isolation)

4. **Security Rule Update (January 2025)**
   - First major update in 20 years
   - Stronger cybersecurity requirements (ransomware defense)
   - Enhanced security controls mandatory
   - **Nillion Advantage:** TEE + attestation meets enhanced standards

**Current State Problems:**

1. **Cloud AI Services See Patient Data**

   - HIPAA requires BAA but provider still has access

2. **On-Premise Solutions Are Expensive**
   - Capital costs: $100K-500K for on-prem AI infrastructure

3. **Data Breach Epidemic**

## Risks & Mitigations

### Risk 1: FDA Clearance Required (HIGH PROBABILITY, HIGH IMPACT)

**Risk:** If classified as diagnostic device (not decision support), FDA 510(k) required

**Impact:**
- Timeline delay: 6-12 months
- Cost: $100K-300K (submission, testing, legal)
- Complexity: Clinical validation studies needed

**Mitigation:**
- **Option A:** Position as Clinical Decision Support System (CDSS) - No FDA clearance needed
  - Requires: Human-in-the-loop (doctor makes final decision)
  - Labeling: "AI-assisted" not "AI diagnostic"
  - Precedent: Many AI tools avoid FDA by being "decision support"

- **Option B:** Pursue FDA 510(k) clearance (Year 2)
  - Advantage: Stronger market position, higher pricing possible
  - Pathway: 510(k) predicate: Existing cleared AI imaging devices
  - Timeline: Start process Month 6, approval Month 12-18

**Recommendation:** Launch as CDSS (Option A), pursue FDA clearance in parallel (Option B) for Year 2

---

### Risk 2: Hospital Sales Cycle (MEDIUM PROBABILITY, MEDIUM IMPACT)

**Risk:** Enterprise sales to hospitals take 6-18 months (slow cash conversion)

**Typical Journey:**
1. Initial contact → 1-2 months
2. Evaluation/RFP → 2-4 months
3. Security review → 1-3 months
4. Legal/contracting → 2-6 months
5. Integration/pilot → 1-3 months
6. Paid contract → Month 7-18

**Mitigation:**
- **Freemium pilot:** Skip procurement for 1,000 free diagnoses (faster decision)
- **Telemedicine first:** Faster sales cycle (30-90 days)
- **Champions:** Find internal advocates (radiologists, CMIO)
- **Published data:** Clinical validation study reduces evaluation time

---

### Risk 3: Integration Complexity (MEDIUM PROBABILITY, LOW IMPACT)

**Risk:** Hospitals require integration with PACS, EHR systems (HL7, FHIR, DICOM)

**Integration Needs:**

- **EHR integration:** Send results to patient record (HL7/FHIR)
- **Workflow:** Fit into radiologist reading workflow

**Mitigation:**
- **Phase 1:** Manual upload (simple, proves value)
- **Phase 2:** DICOM listener (automated image ingestion)
- **Phase 3:** Full EHR integration (HL7/FHIR messages)
- **Partners:** Work with PACS vendors (Merge, Sectra, Intelerad)

**Timeline:** Phase 1 (Month 1), Phase 2 (Month 6), Phase 3 (Month 12)

---

### Risk 4: Clinical Validation Required (HIGH PROBABILITY, MEDIUM IMPACT)

**Risk:** Hospitals want published clinical validation before adoption

**Requirements:**
- Sensitivity/specificity benchmarks
- Comparison to radiologists (inter-rater reliability)
- Published in peer-reviewed journal

**Mitigation:**
- **Retrospective study:** Use public datasets (MIMIC, ChestX-ray14)
- **Academic partnership:** Collaborate with academic medical center
- **Rapid publication:** Submit to open-access journals (PLOS, Nature Digital Medicine)
- **Timeline:** Design study Month 1, data collection Month 2-3, submit Month 4, published Month 6-9

**Cost:** $20K-50K (researcher time, publication fees, data access)
