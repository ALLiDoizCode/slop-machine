# Epic 5: Community Review & Standards Submission

**Status:** Pending
**Priority:** Medium
**Dependencies:**
  - Epic 1 (RFC Specification & Reference Implementation) - Complete RFC document
  - Epic 2 (Smart Contracts) - Deployed contracts as proof of implementation
  - Epic 3 (Multi-Language SDKs) - Multiple implementations validate spec
  - Epic 4 (Demo Applications) - Working demos prove protocol viability
**Estimated Duration:** 4-6 weeks (includes community review cycles)

---

## Epic Goal

Solicit community feedback on the BIMP RFC draft (completed in Epic 1), incorporate revisions based on substantive comments, and submit the specification to IETF or W3C for formal standardization. This epic delivers a community-reviewed, standards-track RFC submission with multiple implementations as proof of concept.

**Value Delivery:** By the end of this epic, BIMP protocol will have:
- Community-reviewed RFC specification with feedback incorporated
- Minimum 10 substantive comments addressed
- Revised RFC draft (v2.0) with clarifications and improvements
- Formal submission to IETF or W3C working group
- Tracking number and acknowledgment from standards body
- Public announcement of RFC submission to BIMP community
